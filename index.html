05( Process )©

A simple process, from first brief to final delivery.

(01)

Brief

We start with your goals, audience, platform and deadline. I collect the footage, brand assets and references I need, so the first draft starts from the right place.

(02)

Plan

I map out the structure: story and pacing for video, layout and style for design, and the right format for each platform, all within your brand guidelines.

(03)

Create

I edit, animate and design, then share drafts early. Your feedback shapes each round of revisions until it feels right.

(04)

Deliver

Final polish on color, sound and details, then exports in the correct sizes and specs for every platform, delivered on time.
